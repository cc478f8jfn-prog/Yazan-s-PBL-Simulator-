About

Yazan's Medplat is a free medical study platform built for medical students, by a medical student. It brings together everything you need for your pre-clinical years in one place:

  • PBL Simulator — 836 clinical cases across Upper Limb, Lower Limb, Embryology and Physiology, with 3 difficulty levels and progress tracking.
  • Video Tutorials — 373 hand-picked lectures from trusted teachers: Dr Ayman Khanfour (Arabic anatomy & embryology), Dr Nagi Iskandar (Arabic physiology) and Ninja Nerd (English).
  • References Books — a library of essential medical textbooks you can read online or download for offline study.

The platform is designed by a medical student who knows exactly what studying feels like — so everything here is built to make learning faster, easier and more effective.
