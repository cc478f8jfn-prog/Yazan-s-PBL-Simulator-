About

Yazan's Medplat is a free medical study platform built for medical students, by a medical student. Everything for the pre-clinical and clinical years in one place, split into a basic-sciences half and a clinical half:

  • PBL Simulator - 1,106 clinical cases organised by body system: musculoskeletal, development, general pathology and every physiology system, with up to 4 difficulty levels and progress tracking. It ships as two simulators: the Basic PBL Simulator for progressive pre-clinical cases, and the OSCE Simulator, where the same cases become scored stations with a virtual patient, focused examination, investigations, a differential and an examiner scorecard.
  • Quiz Bank - 270 MCQs. Basic sciences cover anatomy, physiology, histology, biochemistry, pathology, pharmacology, microbiology and immunology by system; the clinical half covers internal medicine, surgery, paediatrics, obstetrics and gynaecology, and psychiatry. Three levels each.
  • Video Tutorials - 1,280 lectures, Arabic first and English underneath: Dr Ayman Khanfour and Ninja Nerd for anatomy and histology, Dr Nagi Iskandar and Ninja Nerd for physiology, Dr Hesham Esmat for biochemistry, Dr Abdelmoteal Foda for pharmacology, plus Dr Iman Nabil, Osmosis, Zero To Finals, Dr Matt & Dr Mike and Dirty Medicine.

The platform is designed by a medical student who knows exactly what studying feels like, so everything here is built to make learning faster, easier and more effective.
